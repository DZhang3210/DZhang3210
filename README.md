**[Same place or not? What breaks address-matching models](https://dzhang3210.github.io/posts/address-matching/)**
I benchmarked tree models against a siamese BiLSTM, ESIM and DistilBERT on real Overture–Foursquare address pairs, scored by pair type. The surprise: on matches with a missing house number, the simple tree models win clearly.
[Write-up](https://dzhang3210.github.io/posts/address-matching/) · [Full paper (PDF)](https://github.com/DZhang3210/address-matching-by-pair-type/blob/main/paper/address-matching-by-pair-type.pdf) · [Code](https://github.com/DZhang3210/address-matching-by-pair-type)
